🏪 Sari-Sari Store Management System
📌 Overview

The Sari-Sari Store is a small family business that serves the local community by providing affordable everyday essentials such as food, snacks, drinks, and household supplies. It also offers services like prepaid mobile load and product reservations (“pabook”).

🛒 Nature of Business

The store sells basic daily needs including canned goods, instant noodles, snacks, soft drinks, soap, and cleaning products. It also provides:

Prepaid mobile load services
Beverage sales with bottle deposit system
Product reservation (“pabook”) for later pickup
📊 Operations

The store handles around 500 transactions per week, serving regular customer demand for affordable, small-quantity purchases (“tingi system”).

📁 System Scope

The system focuses on:

Sales of basic goods
Load transactions
Supplier deliveries
Customer reservations (“pabook”)
Daily inventory and over-the-counter sales
🧾 Data & Documentation

Daily operations are recorded manually using:

Sales notebook
Load transaction log
Reservation list
Supplier delivery records
📍 Data Source

This system is based on real operations of a family-owned sari-sari store, gathered through direct observation of daily transactions and business processes.
